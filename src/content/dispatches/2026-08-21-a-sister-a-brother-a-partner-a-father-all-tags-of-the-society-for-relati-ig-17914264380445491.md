A sister, a brother, a partner, a father\. All tags of the society for relationships one must always uphold and protect\. I took my time with this specific post as the men and women I recently interviewed hit straight home\. This is by far the most common story shared amongst us humans\. Yet it is also the most misinterpreted one, perhaps giving to the lies surrounding it\. “Oh, you cannot leave her, she’s family\.” “Oh, please you are overreacting, don’t leave\. A far better thing to do is to stay and talk about it and try to fix the situation\. Leaving is easier, but staying and fixing is the right thing to do as everything can be managed by just having a conversation\.” 🥀  

Today’s story is about Tofu, a beautiful and majestic creature\. A cat\. Tofu being just 5 months old is very energetic and filled with energy\. He is in his exploration era where he is constantly examining and testing his environment\. Tofu is loved unconditionally hence he is flourishing\. Sometimes climbing the tree, constantly moving and pouncing\. Seeing a bee and getting in a hunting position\. He is basically living life freely as intended\. 🐈🌿  

Now what would happen if suddenly Tofu became a human being? A delightful young boy or a girl\. And now he had parents, siblings, everyone\. What would happen if Tofu had a very narcissistic sister? What would happen if she’d have a narcissistic father, mother, brother or anyone else for that matter? What would happen if since birth such elements have always existed in Tofu’s life? What would Tofu’s life look like now? 🌑  

You look at any human life, and you can tell their current selves are the byproduct of years of specific conditioning, their friends, society, etc\. Every life is as dense and complicated as any layer in an ocean or a river\. There is no right or wrong, just complications within complications\. How could a single conversation or rather even a few take away the damage done over decades of time?
