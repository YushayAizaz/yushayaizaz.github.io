We sat on the grass as the evening light began to fade, a notebook between us and a quiet understanding in the air\. 🌿  

Today, I spoke with two women in their early twenties\. What started as a lighter conversation under the trees quickly deepened into an exploration of the invisible weight they carry\. 🍂  

We talked about the quiet, pervasive aspects of patriarchy that have shaped their realities\. We unpacked the exhaustion of past relationships—the profound damage done by dating emotionally unaware partners\. 🥀  

People who, instead of confronting their own shadows, projected their unaddressed traumas onto their partners, draining their energy and leaving them to pick up the pieces\. 🌑  

But as I listened to them map out these heavy, damaging experiences, I didn't just see the scars they carried\. I saw a defiant, incredible resilience\. In their eyes, if I felt a slight hesitation and fear, I also saw at times hints of their own power\. ✨  

I saw two individuals who have been hurt by the people they trusted, yet refuse to become bitter\. They haven't given up\. They are actively doing the work, healing, setting boundaries, and fiercely striving to build better lives for themselves\. 🌱  

To the people who are out there healing from things that were never your fault: your strength is profoundly beautiful\. 🤍  

Thank you for trusting me with your stories this wonderful evening\. 📖🌆
