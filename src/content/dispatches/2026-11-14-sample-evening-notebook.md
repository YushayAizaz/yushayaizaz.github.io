This is demonstration prose for the publishing system, not a reported interview or a finished piece by Yushay Aizaz.

At the edge of an evening, a notebook opens. The first task is not to find the most beautiful sentence. It is to make enough room for another person to finish theirs.

A field dispatch can begin here and grow into a longer essay. Once this file exists, the Instagram importer will never revise it.
