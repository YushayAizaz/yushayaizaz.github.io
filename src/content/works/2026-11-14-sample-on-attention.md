Attention begins with a small decision: to remain with what another person is saying before preparing an answer. A conversation is not only an exchange of information. It is also an agreement, renewed sentence by sentence, that a life deserves to be heard.

## A practice of listening

The page makes room for headings, quotations, links and unhurried paragraphs. A published essay can use these forms without surrendering its own rhythm.
