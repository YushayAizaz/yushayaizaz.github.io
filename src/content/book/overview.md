I began the evening interviews because I wanted to hear people tell their own stories. The book grows out of that practice. It asks what draws us toward one another, what we find hard to put into words, and what it takes to listen when another person’s account of their life surprises us.

Studying computer science has taught me to look for patterns. My interest in psychology has made me curious about how people understand the patterns in their own lives. The writing brings those questions to the particulars of a conversation: a detail someone comes back to, an account that changes as it is told, or a moment they remember differently from everyone else who was there.

I’m still gathering stories. This page will follow the book as it takes shape.
